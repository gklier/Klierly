# Study on your phone

Open a course and choose **Study**, or tap **Start studying** from its overview. The mobile navigation keeps Study, Plan, Practice and Materials within reach. More contains the remaining course tools.

Choose a short round and check its module scope. If you have an exam plan, its modules are preselected. Specific topics narrow that selection further. A round chooses up to the requested number of concepts, prioritizing recent misses and then concepts you have not tried.

## Two modes

**Learn mix** interleaves recall, recognition, explanation and application. Application turns require an existing active question in that topic's bank; use Practice to generate more from permitted sources if needed. Recognition alternatives come from the selected scope. With only one possible concept, the reverse card is self-assessed instead of presenting a one-option quiz.

**Flashcards** prompts you to recall and explain before turning the card. Say the answer aloud or use the optional scratch space. The scratch draft remains on the current screen and is not uploaded or saved. Compare against the reference, then mark Remembered or Review again. On a touch device, you can also swipe the reference right for remembered and left for review; buttons remain available for accessibility.

Misses return later in a different format, when enough intervening turns remain. A round adds no more than four retries and does not retry a retry indefinitely. Recent misses are prioritized in later rounds. Notes and references use existing course content; starting a round does not make a provider call.

## What the recap means

Question answers are checked on the server. Free recall and explanations in your own words are **self-assessed**, not semantically graded by AI. The recap separates those counts. Angle checkmarks show successful turns, including self-assessments, and are not a certification of mastery. Study rounds do not silently inflate the existing exam-readiness score.

## Saved rounds and source changes

Turns are saved to your account, so the same round can resume after refresh or on another signed-in device. If a save fails, the screen reports it and offers a retry/reload. Starting a new round ends the previous active round for that course but retains its recap. You can pause by leaving the runner and resume from Study.

Queues contain topic/question references and outcomes, not copies of document text. If a source, question or topic is removed, the app does not invent a replacement; start a fresh round with current material. Course or account deletion cascades to its study rounds.

An internet connection is required. There is no offline service-worker cache of private material. The web manifest enables a standalone home-screen presentation in supporting browsers; passkey compatibility depends on the device, browser and credential provider.
