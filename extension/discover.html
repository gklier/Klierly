Discover this course

Klierly reads the Modules page in your course tab, shows you what it found, and imports only what you select and approve. It opens each selected item in that tab, one at a time, the same way you would by clicking.

Allow access to this course site

To move through the course pages you choose, the extension needs your permission for only. Chrome will ask you to confirm. You can remove it again at any time below or in Chrome's extension settings.

It does not read passwords, cookies, grades, people lists, quizzes or anything on other sites.

Class library (optional). Share AI summaries with classmates and read theirs. Joining tells Klierly that you opened this Canvas course: its number, address and name, nothing else. It does not confirm enrolment, so only share summaries you are comfortable with anyone in the class seeing.
