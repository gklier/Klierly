Klierly Capture settings

Connect the extension to your own Klierly server. It never asks for your LMS login, your Klierly password, or any access token from your university.

Use https:// unless Klierly runs on this computer (localhost).

Create it in Klierly under Settings → Browser extension. It can only list your courses and add imports, and you can revoke it there at any time. It is stored only in this browser.

What this extension does

  • Works only when you click its toolbar button, and only on the tab you are looking at.
  • Shows you the exact text before anything is sent, and sends it only after you click Import.
  • Leaves out navigation, hidden content, form fields, embedded frames, grades and class lists.
  • Refuses pages that look like a quiz or exam in progress, grades pages and people pages.
  • Does not read cookies, passwords, other tabs or network traffic, and does not run in the background.
